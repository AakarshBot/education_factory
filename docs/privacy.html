Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Exam Session India Factory handles Google user data when the factory is authorized to manage the associated YouTube channel.

Google data accessed

The factory requests YouTube upload access to upload and schedule videos to the channel, and read-only YouTube Analytics access to retrieve channel and video performance metrics.

How data is used

Google data is used only to perform channel publishing and analytics functions for the channel owner. It is not sold or used for unrelated advertising.

Storage

OAuth credentials and tokens are stored locally by the channel owner's factory environment and are not committed to the public source repository.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold or shared with third parties except where required for the requested API operation.
