Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes the handling of information by Exam Session India Factory, software operated locally by the owner of the Exam Session India YouTube channel.

Information handled

The factory may handle Google account authorization information, YouTube channel and video data, publicly available YouTube demand signals, and YouTube Analytics data needed to operate and improve the channel.

How information is used

Information is used to authenticate the channel owner, upload and manage channel videos, manage video metadata, and evaluate channel performance. YouTube data is not used to identify or profile unrelated third-party individuals.

Storage and sharing

The factory is operated locally. OAuth credentials, tokens, generated content, production files, and analytics history may be stored on the operator's local computer for the operation of the factory. The operator does not sell personal information. Data is not intentionally shared with unrelated third parties.

The factory also sends the content-generation prompts and related educational production inputs required for content creation to its configured AI service provider.

YouTube API Services

The factory's use of YouTube API Services is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and YouTube API Services Terms of Service.

Revoking access

The channel owner can revoke the factory's Google authorization from the Google Account security settings or by removing the local OAuth token used by the factory.

Google Privacy Policy

Google's Privacy Policy is available at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Contact

For questions about this policy, contact the channel owner through the Google account associated with Exam Session India.
